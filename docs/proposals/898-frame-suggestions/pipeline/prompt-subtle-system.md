# Subtle system reference (from wemeditate.com)

Source is the We Meditate site only. The site gives few body locations, no mantras and almost no affirmations; where it is silent this file says "not on site". Use the qualities below to infer chakra and side from a narrator's wording.

Hand rule (hand-on-chakra page): use the hand on the OPPOSITE side of the body from the chakra (left-side chakra -> right hand, and vice versa); the other hand rests on the lap, palm up. Hold hand and attention on the chakra for some minutes; an affirmation or prayer may be said silently, e.g. "Mother, I am communicative and expressive." Throat: right hand on throat. Mooladhara: right hand on or toward the earth.

## Mooladhara (Muladhara) - first chakra
- Where: pelvic plexus, below the sacrum where Kundalini lies dormant; start of the left channel.
- Core quality: innocence (no hidden motive or wish for personal gain, like a child); balanced priorities; intuitive sense of the right path, decisions without second-guessing, joyful enjoyment of life, wise discrimination.
- Left: chastity, rightful conduct; sex as an expression of love but not dominating desires and attention; clear attention and pure wish to grow.
- Right: dynamism and discipline; imbalance = rigid, over-strict, no joy. Balanced by spontaneous joy, "innocence in action", effortless action.
- Centre: no separate description (innocence).
- Deity: Shri Ganesha, the primordial child, innocent, wise, remover of obstacles. Earth connection (hand toward earth).

## Swadhisthan (Swadisthan, Swadhisthana) - second chakra
- Where: central aspect linked to the aortic plexus; right aspect to the liver (with right Nabhi). Left: no location on site.
- Left: pure knowledge (truth about oneself, revealed by Kundalini awakening); pure desire (growth toward one's true self).
- Centre: pure creativity ("seat of creativity"); ideas from inspiration, not ego or conditioning (art, science, problem-solving).
- Right: pure attention; seat of the attention needed for meditation; with right Nabhi governs the liver (feeds glucose to the brain). Blocked by a crowded mind and scattered attention; cleared by surrendering thoughts.
- Cooling: ice pack on the liver.

## Nabhi (Manipur) - third chakra, centre of the Void
- Where: abdomen, at the centre of the Void; right Nabhi over the liver beside right Swadhisthan.
- Left: sustenance of family and household; home peace, emotional stability, love, respect, unity. Blocked by selfishness, greed, avoiding family duties.
- Centre: overall satisfaction ("make me a satisfied person"); from basic needs to deeper fulfilment; peace not tied to wealth; generosity.
- Right: mental satisfaction; worry and constant thinking overheat the liver; irritability, stress. Putting concerns in perspective gives a calm, dignified presence.
- Symbols: Hand of Fatima (against selfishness and greed); Lakshmi, Athena as preserver archetypes (not named as the chakra's deity).

## Void / Bhavasagara - the abdominal region around the Nabhi
- Where: abdominal cavity; Nabhi at its centre, Swadhisthan rotating around as its outer edge. Centre of self-mastery.
- Nabhi = satisfaction, Swadhisthan = attention; attention fixed on money, possessions, family, work or achievements blocks it.
- Left: discerning right from wrong, conviction in own decisions; intuition ("gut feeling"), decisiveness without ego, feeling subtle energy at the fingertips. Blocked by self-doubt, being dominated.
- Right: living by dharma (righteous conduct that sustains life); sustainability, balanced care for self and others. Blocked by dominating or abusing others or oneself.
- Opening: believe you can be your own master, avoid fanaticism and extremes, avoid alcohol and drugs.

## Heart (Anahat, Anahata) - fourth chakra
- Where: not stated on site (chest/heart area by common sense only). Hand-on-chakra page links heart to confidence, fearlessness, courage; no placement given.
- Left: the spirit, the purest self ("I am the spirit"); freedom from identifying with thoughts and emotions; unconditional love, no dependence on circumstances for happiness. Self-love, kindness to oneself.
- Centre: security, compassion, protection; fear constricts it, self-confidence strengthens; natural defences (mental, emotional, physical). Reject fear as an illusion.
- Right: responsibility and duty from love for others; pride in roles (work, family); even menial duties as love; imbalance = irresponsible or over-responsible.
- Figure: Durga, protective fearless love (not explicitly tied to the chakra).

## Vishuddhi (Vishuddha) - fifth chakra
- Where: throat (right hand on the throat); body location otherwise not stated on its page.
- Left: self-respect; guilt and low self-esteem wear it down; accept mistakes, learn, move on; pure brother-sister relationships raise it.
- Centre: collectivity ("everyone is part of one whole", beyond tribe, nation, land) and detached witness stance, enjoying life as a play like a theatre; discretion about when and what to speak. Comes from an open heart and sweet, joyful relationships.
- Right: communication; speaking sweetly and respectfully; diplomacy ("tongue sweet like honey"); sarcasm blocks it.
- Figure: Lord Krishna, the "divine diplomat" (not explicitly the deity).
- Affirmation on site: "Mother, I am communicative and expressive."

## Agnya (Agya) - sixth chakra
- Where: not stated on site (centre of the forehead/brow area by common usage only). The left and right channels end here: left in the superego, right in the ego.
- Left Agnya (superego): stores past conditioning (family, friends, nationality, upbringing); blocked = self-critical, feeling a failure. Cleared = present, guided by the Spirit, free of the past.
- Central: forgiveness and humility ("I forgive everyone"); forgive self and others so thoughts ease; mildness as strength; present without thought.
- Right Agnya (ego): ideas about oneself (identity, achievements, opinions); blocked = superiority, "I do a lot"; nourished = surrender, humility. We are all pure spirit, therefore equal.
- Figure: Jesus ("Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do").
- Hamsa and Back Agnya: not on site.

## Sahasrara - seventh chakra
- Where: top of the head, fontanel area, limbic area of the brain; thousand-petalled flower, a bud before Kundalini awakening.
- Integration of all chakras and their qualities; thoughtless awareness, bliss, quiet mind, duality and separation overcome; a "cool breeze" felt at the crown when open; a stronghold against negative thoughts and weak states.
- Practices: redirect attention to the top of the head when thoughts turn negative; massage crown with palm (scalp clockwise); coconut or almond oil before bed; cover head in winter. Palm on the fontanel area. Counter a negative thought with a positive one or say "Stop!"

## Kundalini
- Dormant energy in the sacrum (triangular bone at the spine base); rises up the spine through the chakras, exits at the fontanelle (cool breeze). Sensations felt in the palms; each part of the hand corresponds to a chakra.

## Channels
- Left channel (Ida): desires, emotions, the past. Balanced = deep emotions, unconditional love, peace, joy. Left-sided = dwelling on the past, sentimental, down-hearted, lethargic; or shutting emotions off. Ends in the superego (left Agnya). Elements: fire (candle flame), earth (sit on the ground, outdoors, sunshine), water (saltwater foot soak). Body side: site does not state it; it is part of the central nervous system. Hand-on-chakra rule makes the right hand work on left-side chakras.
- Right channel (Pingala): thoughts, actions, the future. Right side of the central nervous system; the liver supports it. Healthy = efficient, spontaneous, joyful action. Overactive = planning, irritability, overheating, can't relax. Ends in the ego (right Agnya). Balance by cooling: ice pack on the liver, saltwater foot soak, shower, water, sleep, less screen time. Model: Hanuman (stays calm and finds another way).
- Central channel (Sushumna): ascent and search for truth; contentment and purpose, innate morality, centred personality without extremes; middle path in dilemmas; focus on own conduct, not judging others. Kundalini rises through it to the Sahasrara. Linked to thoughtless awareness, bliss, collective unconscious.
- Balancing phrasing such as "raise the left hand to the sky to balance the right side" is NOT on the site; the site only gives cooling/candle/foot-soak practices above.

## Quality-to-chakra lookup
innocence -> Mooladhara; creativity -> centre Swadhisthan; attention -> right Swadhisthan; self-knowledge/true desire -> left Swadhisthan; satisfaction -> centre Nabhi; family/home -> left Nabhi; worry/mental satisfaction -> right Nabhi; gut feeling/discernment -> left Void; dharma/sustainability -> right Void; spirit/unconditional love -> left Heart; security/fearlessness/compassion -> centre Heart; duty/responsibility -> right Heart; self-respect/guilt -> left Vishuddhi; collectivity/witness -> centre Vishuddhi; communication/sweet speech -> right Vishuddhi; past/conditioning/self-criticism -> left Agnya; forgiveness/humility -> centre Agnya; ego/superiority -> right Agnya; thoughtless awareness/cool breeze -> Sahasrara.
